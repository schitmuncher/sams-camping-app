    How the stacking maths works

    Sleep rating starts at your sleeping bag's comfort temperature. Each extra layer lowers it by its warmth bonus (each item counted once). Shelters add a little: tent +3°C, bivvy +2°C, tarp +1°C (max +5°C).

    Ground R-value: mat R-values add when stacked. Target: ≥10°C → R1, 5–10°C → R2, 0–5°C → R3, −5–0°C → R4, below −5°C → R5 (hammocks at least R2). Each point short adds ~3°C.

    Wind chill: the average night wind is reduced by your best shelter's wind rating (3/3 blocks it all). What gets through costs about 1°C per 4 mph, up to 5°C.

    Damp: humidity of 90%+ or rain costs 1°C (synthetic fill loses a little loft). Rain your shelter can't handle costs another 2°C.

    Your calibration: once you log trips on the Trips tab, the app learns whether you sleep warmer or colder than the maths says and adjusts.

    Limit: below the bag's comfort rating but above its limit, you'll get through the night curled up but sleep badly. Below the limit is dangerous.

    Camp height: when a route is loaded, each camp's forecast uses its elevation from the GPX. Air cools about 0.65°C per 100 m, so a hilltop camp can be noticeably colder than the valley.

    Verdict: rating above the low = cold. More than 8°C below the low = you're carrying more warmth (and weight) than needed.

    📓 Log a night out

    After a trip, record how warm you actually slept. The app compares it with what it predicted and learns your personal offset.

    📲 Install on your phone

    Open the GitHub Pages link in your phone browser. On Android Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or the Install button here when it appears). On iPhone Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. After that it works offline, though the forecast needs signal.

    Export

    CSV opens in Excel / Google Sheets. JSON can be imported back into this app on any device.

    Import

    Accepts a full backup, a kit list, or a single class file. A class file is added to your classes; a kit list merges new items in by name.

    Reset

    Your data is saved on this device only (trip log included). Make a backup before clearing browser data or switching phones.
