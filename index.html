Warnings & tips

    How the stacking maths works

    Sleep rating starts at your sleeping bag's comfort temperature. Each extra layer (thermals, gaiter, gloves) lowers it by its warmth bonus. Shelters add a little: tent +3°C, bivvy +2°C, tarp +1°C (capped at +5°C combined).

    Ground R-value: sleeping-mat R-values add together when stacked. Target R for the night: ≥10°C → R1, 5–10°C → R2, 0–5°C → R3, −5–0°C → R4, below −5°C → R5. Each point you're short adds about 3°C to your sleep rating (you'll feel the cold from below). Hammocks need R2+ underneath or you lose a lot of heat.

    Verdict: if your rating is above the overnight low, you'll be cold. If it's more than 8°C below the low you're carrying more warmth (and weight) than needed.

    Ratings marked "est." are estimates — budget gear rarely has official EN/ISO ratings. Edit any item to put in your own numbers.
